Kids and Families

Our 16-inch kids bikes are suited for children roughly 3 to 6 years old (around 35 to 48 inches tall). The 20-inch bikes suit ages 5 to 8, and the 24-inch bikes are right for ages 7 to 11. If your child is not quite ready to ride solo, we have tag-along options and a kids tricycle.

Two great options. The Burley Trailer attaches to your bike and seats up to two small children; they ride along while you pedal. The Baby Seat Bicycle has a built-in child seat on the frame, ideal for one younger child (adults must be at least 5'2" to ride comfortably). Both are included or available as part of a rental package.

Both are tag-along attachments that hitch to the rear of your adult bike. The Wee HOO puts your child in a lower, recumbent-style seat and is best for ages 3 to 8. The Tug A Bug is more upright, with its own seat and handlebars, and suits ages 3 to 9 who are a little more confident. Both are available exclusively at Peddlers on 30A. Not sure which fits your child? Call us and we'll help you decide.

Yes. The Kazoo Kids Tandem is a two-person bike with a parent in front and a child co-pilot behind in a stoker seat. The Bike + Burley Combo pairs a full adult bike with a Burley trailer for small kids. And for families where everyone rides their own bike, we have matching sets in multiple sizes. We can put together a full family fleet, just let us know your group at booking.

Yes. Helmets are included with every full bike rental, including kids bikes. Florida law requires helmets for cyclists under 16. We make it easy; yours is ready when you pick up.

Section 6

Riding on 30A

Yes. The paved path along Scenic Highway 30A is flat the entire way, no hills, no gravel. It runs 19 miles total from Camp Helen State Park in the east to Miramar Beach in the west. Most fitness levels handle it without any trouble. Kids, seniors, and casual riders all enjoy it. The biggest variable is heat; plan your ride early in the morning or after 4pm in summer.

We are located right on Scenic Highway 30A in Seacrest Beach. Alys Beach is about a mile east. Rosemary Beach is roughly 1.2 miles east. Heading west, Seagrove Beach, WaterColor, and Seaside are all reachable along the paved path. For covering the western half of 30A, a 7-speed bike gives you more range. Ask us when you pick up and we'll map out the best route for your group.

Early morning, before 9am, is the best window in summer. The trail is quiet, the air is cool, and the light is something else. The stretch from Seacrest Beach east to Rosemary Beach before breakfast is one of the best rides on the Florida Panhandle. If you're riding later in the day, plan for shade stops and bring water. After 4pm is the second-best window once the heat breaks.

Florida law requires helmets for riders under 16. We strongly recommend helmets for riders of all ages. Helmets are included with every full bike rental at Peddlers, so there's nothing extra to bring or buy.
