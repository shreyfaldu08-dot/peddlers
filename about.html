OUR STORY

Built Around the Way 30A Moves

Peddlers began in 2011 with a simple idea: help people slow down and see 30A the way locals do. From Seacrest Beach, riders could leave the parking lot behind and explore the coast at a pace that made every stop worth taking.

Over the years, Peddlers has grown from a bike rental shop into a gathering place for visitors and locals alike. Right on Scenic Highway 30A in Seacrest Beach, it has become a familiar starting point for beach rides, morning coffee runs, and evenings spent together after a day on the coast.

Know the Coast Like a Local
