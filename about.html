About Peddlers 30A

The gateway to the Emerald Coast. We curate more than just rides; we curate the soul of your coastal escape.

THE HEART OF THE COAST

Crafting Memories Since the Beginning

Peddlers 30A began with a simple vision: to connect people with the natural beauty of the Florida Panhandle in the most authentic way possible — on two wheels.

What started as a boutique bike rental has evolved into a cornerstone of the 30A lifestyle. We believe that the best way to see our coastal villages is at a pace that allows you to notice the shifting light on the dunes and the scent of jasmine on the breeze.
